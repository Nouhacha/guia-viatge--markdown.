# Mini Travel Guide: My Favorite City - Barcelona

Hello! My name is **Nouha**. I am a *serious*, *responsible*, and *helpful* person who always strives to support the people around me. I take my commitments seriously, value organization, and love exploring new places while learning about different cultures.

In this small guide, I would like to introduce you to my favorite city: *Barcelona*.

---

## Points of Interest & My Top 3
If you come to visit me, here is my **Top 3** list of essential places you must see:

1. **Sagrada Família Basilica**: Antoni Gaudí's masterwork, an *architectural* wonder.
2. **Park Güell**: A beautiful park full of nature, vibrant colors, and incredible city views.
3. **Gothic Quarter**: The historic heart of the city, perfect for walking through *charming* streets.

---

## Local Food & What to Pack
As an *organized* person, here are the essential things you should bring and try:

* **Pa amb tomàquet (Bread with Tomato)**: A traditional, simple, and *delicious* local dish.
* **Comfortable shoes**: Essential for walking around and discovering the city.
* **Sunglasses**: Barcelona enjoys *sunny* weather almost all year round.
* **A positive attitude**: To enjoy the trip and always be ready to help your travel buddies.
